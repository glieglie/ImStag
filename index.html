THE STAG ESCAPE

ROOM 1 — THE CLASSROOM

The bell has rung.

The classroom is empty.

THE BLACKBOARD

Someone has written an equation on the board:

12 + 7 × 3 − 5

Solve it. The answer may be important.

🔓 CABINET UNLOCKED

Inside the cabinet you find an old piece of paper.

THE TEACHER'S NOTE

"The first number is not the answer."

"Look at the equation again."

"Count the numbers."

"That number will open the next lock."

THE SECOND LOCK

How many numbers appear in the equation?

12 + 7 × 3 − 5

🚪 CLASSROOM DOOR

You hear a click.

The lock has opened.
